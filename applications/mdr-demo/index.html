NKP Demo App

This is the landing page for the nginx demo application from the MDR Catalog. It runs as an nginx Deployment on your NKP cluster and serves this page from a Kubernetes ConfigMap.

Nothing here talks to the rest of your cluster. Its only job is to prove the path from ingress to pod works, so you have something real to point a browser at.

How this page reached you

Ingressmdr-demo, kommander-traefik, path /mdr-demo, TLS terminates here
Servicemdr-demo, ClusterIP :80, selects app=mdr-demo-nginx
Deploymentmdr-demo, keeps the nginx pods running and labelled
Podsnginx:1.31-alpine, container port 80, one of them answered
ConfigMapmdr-demo-index-page, mounted read-only at /usr/share/nginx/html/mdr-demo
This pageyou are here

Check it from a terminal

kubectl get pods -n <workspace-namespace> -l app=mdr-demo-nginx
kubectl get deploy/mdr-demo svc/mdr-demo ingress/mdr-demo -n <workspace-namespace>
kubectl describe configmap mdr-demo-index-page -n <workspace-namespace>
kubectl port-forward -n <workspace-namespace> svc/mdr-demo 8080:80
kubectl logs -n <workspace-namespace> -l app=mdr-demo-nginx --tail=20

Swap <workspace-namespace> for the namespace of the NKP workspace you installed into. After a port-forward the page is at localhost:8080/mdr-demo/, not the root.
